# Phase 3 — Rotation

1. Selection is by eligibility time first, always; the D2 tie-break is applied exactly.
2. Every recommendation carries a human-readable explanation (who, why, how long they waited).
3. A player can never appear in two active games; unavailable players/courts are never assigned.
4. Declined-turn policy D1 is the default and the alternatives are selectable per session.
5. Skill balancing (D6) and partner rotation (D7) are off by default, togglable, and never drop the oldest-waiting player.
6. Manual overrides require and store a reason in a RotationEvent.
7. An incomplete court stays open rather than receiving an invalid assignment.
